module Base

    /*
     * Katana: the vanilla definition (Build 42.21) with three changes.
     *
     * 1. Tags += base:sharpenable
     *    Without this tag the katana is the ONLY long blade in the game that
     *    cannot be sharpened: the "Sharpen Blade" recipe (whetstone, file or
     *    grindstone) asks for an item with the base:sharpenable tag, and the
     *    katana does not have it. Machete, sword, knives and axes all do.
     *
     * 2. ConditionMax 10 -> 13 (same as the sword and the machete)
     *    The edge (Sharpness) wears off by 1/ConditionMax per hit, and the
     *    ConditionMax is also how long the weapon lasts: with 10 the katana
     *    broke before any other blade.
     *
     * 3. ConditionLowerChanceOneIn 15 -> 20 (same as the sword)
     *    Chance of a hit damaging the weapon.
     *
     * WHY THE EDGE MATTERS: the engine scales melee damage by
     * (Sharpness + 1) / 2. With a pristine edge (1.0) the damage is 100%, with
     * a dull edge (0.0) it is 50%. Since the katana could not be sharpened, a
     * dulled one stayed at 50% forever.
     */
    item Katana
